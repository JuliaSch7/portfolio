Crone Cyclone

Riso Animation

An exploration into the perception and construction of the word "crone" by reconfiguring and creating new word associations based on popular phrases, metaphors, and proverbs. This work aims to reveal the negative perception through which society views older women and how older women are often pigeonholed into two categories: cruel crone or a gentle granny.

Notorious RGB RBG

Risograph

This design response, "Notorious RGB RBG, Riso", distorts the image and words of Ruth Bader Ginsburg, unraveling the veneer of the ‘Cult of Personality’ that elevates her into an untouchable icon. The work probes the tension between reverence and reality through layered riso visuals and warped text, excerpts from some of her most famous quotes, exposing the dangers of flattening a complex figure into a saintly caricature. It questions how society constructs and risks losing the humanity behind the myth.

Crone Abstraction I

Risograph

These scans were further altered and abstracted, marked by rippling, wave-like distortions that further fragment the visual plane. Building on the deconstruction of Ruth Bader Ginsburg’s image, this progression strips away familiar iconography to challenge the viewer’s perception of identity and mythology. By employing riso printing, scanning, and digital distortion, the series defamiliarizes static representations, inviting a reimagining and redefinition of the Crone as a fluid, multifaceted figure that defies societal constraints and expectations.
