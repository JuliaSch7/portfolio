Linguistics Collection

An exploration into the perception and construction of the word "crone" by reconfiguring and creating new word associations based on popular phrases, metaphors, and proverbs.

This work aims to reveal the negative perception through which society views older women and how older women are often pigeonholed into two categories: cruel crone or a gentle granny.

Crone Abstractions

The imagery of the Crone was created by abstracting the aging female form through the use of riso, scanners, and digital methods.

Read more ↓

These scans were further altered and abstracted, marked by rippling, wave-like distortions that further fragment the visual plane. By defamiliarizing the viewer with the typical forms associated with old age, we can reimagine the crone's identity as more complex and fluid, transcending society's expectations.
