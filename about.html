About

Hello,
I'm Julia.

Julia Schirrmeister

Graphic Designer & Artist · New York City

I design with intention. Across UX, brand identity, editorial, and print, my work is research-driven, built on the belief that good design asks questions before it answers them.

My background in scholarly research shaped how I approach design problems. Pratt's MFA program in Communication Design formalized that approach, and it's core to how I work. I don't start with the visual. I start with context: who the audience is, what ideas are actually at stake, and what the work needs to accomplish. The aesthetics follow from that process, not the other way around.

Right now, I'm looking for full-time graphic design or UX opportunities in New York City, at organizations where communication, people, and culture intersect. I work well independently and in teams, in environments where rigor and curiosity aren't treated as opposites.

When I'm not designing, I'm creating art or reading. Drawing, painting, working through a book; the edge between image and language is where I thrive.
