Poster Series

An experimental typography poster series investigating language as visual form — pushing at the boundary between legibility and image. Each poster in the series treats a single word or phrase as its subject, asking: what happens to meaning when form takes over?

Designer
Illustrator, InDesign
Typography / Print
2022
01

Concept & Narrative

Typography has always lived in the tension between communication and form. At maximum legibility, a typeface is nearly invisible — a transparent vessel for meaning. At maximum expressiveness, it becomes image, pattern, texture — something felt before it is read.

This series explores that spectrum deliberately. Each poster is designed around a single constraint: the letterform must do double duty — functioning simultaneously as language and as visual structure. The viewer reads and sees at once, and the two acts interfere with each other productively.

"At what point does a word stop being read and start being seen?"

The series consists of six posters, each treating a different word — chosen for semantic resonance with its visual treatment. The typefaces, scale, and composition change across each poster while the conceptual framework remains constant.

02

Research & References

Research drew on Concrete Poetry, Swiss International Style, and contemporary typographic experimentation — from Emigré-era deconstruction to the current resurgence of variable font expression. Key references included work by Wolfgang Weingart, Niklaus Troxler, and Experimental Jetset.

03

Process & Iterations

Each poster went through multiple rounds of iteration — starting with hand sketches that worked out the visual logic of each word before moving to digital. The critical question at each stage: does the visual treatment illuminate the word, or merely decorate it?

04

Typeface Studies

Typeface selection was treated as a primary design decision, not a finishing choice. Each poster uses a different typeface selected for how its structural qualities (stroke contrast, terminal shape, weight distribution) interact with the meaning of its word.

05

Final Posters

The six final posters, each 18 × 24 inches, printed on 100lb uncoated cover stock.

Final Outcome & Reflection

The poster series is the most purely typographic work in my portfolio — and the project that most directly reflects my interest in language as a designed object. Typography, at its most ambitious, isn't a tool for communication; it is the communication.

What I learned: Working with such tight constraints — one word, one surface — stripped away every easy solution. The difficulty forced genuine inventiveness. The best work in this series came from asking "what else can this letterform be?" and refusing to stop at the first good answer.
